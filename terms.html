What Wexel is

Wexel converts prices between currencies using your camera or manual entry. It is an informational tool, not a financial service.

Rates are estimates

Exchange rates come from a public mid-market rates feed and may be cached on your device for offline use. Actual rates offered by banks, card issuers, or exchange bureaus will differ. Wexel shows when its rates were last updated, but you should not rely on the app for financial decisions, and we accept no liability for losses arising from displayed rates.

Free tier and subscriptions

  • The free tier includes 1 camera scan per day. Manual entry is unlimited.
  • Wexel Pro (unlimited scans) is an auto-renewable subscription: $2.99/month or $19.99/year in the US, with a 3-day free trial. In other countries the App Store charges the equivalent price in your local currency, shown before you subscribe.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it anytime in your device Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • After the trial or a cancelled subscription, the app returns to the free tier — it keeps working.
