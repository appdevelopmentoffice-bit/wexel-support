Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

Wexel doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

What the camera sees

Wexel uses your iPhone's camera to recognize prices. All text recognition happens on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework. Camera images are never stored, and never leave your phone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your last-used currency pair
  • Cached exchange rates, so conversion works offline
  • Your daily free-scan count and whether you've seen the intro screens
  • Whether you've turned sound effects on or off

This information lives only on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network requests

The app periodically downloads current exchange rates from a public rates API. This request contains no personal information — it's the same for every user.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. "Restore Purchases" is an on-device receipt check with Apple — no account with us is involved, because none exists.

Children

Wexel does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
