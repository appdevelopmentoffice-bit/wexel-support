Common questions

How many scans do I get for free?

One camera scan every day, forever — it isn't a trial that expires. A scan covers a whole recognition session, so refining the same price doesn't cost you extra. Manual entry is always unlimited, even without a subscription.

The camera isn't reading a price

Center the price inside the scan window and hold steady for a moment. Very glossy tags, handwriting, and unusual fonts can defeat text recognition — tap the keyboard icon to type the amount instead. Wexel handles small raised cents (like 6995), thousands separators, and currency symbols automatically.

A converted amount looks wrong

Check the two currency chips at the bottom of the screen — the left one is the currency you're scanning, the right one is your own. Tap either to change it, or tap the arrows between them to swap. Rates are mid-market and will differ from what a bank or exchange bureau actually offers.

Do rates work offline?

Yes. Wexel caches the latest rates on your device and keeps converting without a connection. If the cached rates are more than a day old, an amber warning appears so you know they may have drifted.

How do I cancel Wexel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by Wexel. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Wexel, then Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. When it lapses, the app returns to the free tier and keeps working.

I paid before but Pro isn't showing

Open the rate pill at the top of the scan screen to reach the About sheet, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you originally purchased with.

What does Wexel collect about me?

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Text recognition runs entirely on your iPhone and camera images never leave the device. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I turn the sounds off?

Yes — open the About sheet from the rate pill and switch off Sound effects. Your iPhone's silent switch mutes them too.
